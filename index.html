06 // QUESTIONS & CONTRACT CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with me on your next project.

What is your typical project turnaround?

Most client projects range between 2 to 5 weeks depending on scope. Rapid MVPs and 3D landing pages can be delivered in as little as 10–14 business days, while enterprise machine learning pipelines typically take 4–6 weeks.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes, absolutely. Proprietary intellectual property, novel AI architectures, and confidential client assets are treated with strict confidentiality. I routinely sign mutual NDAs prior to in-depth technical scoping.

How do we communicate and track sprint progress?

I embed directly into your preferred workflow: dedicated Slack or Discord channels, weekly live video demos, and continuous GitHub pull requests with clear documentation. You always know the exact status of your deliverable.

What happens after the project launches?

Every project includes 30 days of complimentary post-launch support and bug fixes. Retainer options and ongoing advisory are also available for continuous feature iteration.
